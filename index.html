concept project

Concept project: “Table for Two”, a fictional product created for portfolio purposes. It shows how one message, “I saved you a seat,” can work across product naming, a product page, packaging, email, social and a designer brief.

The idea

  • Product: A pop-up greeting card that opens to reveal a cozy kitchen table set for two. A small candle glows in the middle, with two empty chairs waiting on either side.
  • Core Message: I saved you a seat.

Note: This image is for illustrative purposes only and does not depict a real scene.

The idea

  • Audience: People who want to say “you matter to me” but struggle to find the words, or don’t want to make a speech out of it.
  • Moments: Thank-yous, birthdays, anniversaries, and “I miss you” notes to someone far away.
  • Why it works: An empty chair is a simple image everyone understands. It says “you’re welcome here” without a single word.

Product
Page (PDP)

  • Product name: Table for Two
  • Tagline: For the person who always saves you a seat.
  • Description: A pop-up greeting card that opens to reveal a cozy kitchen table set for two, complete with a glowing candle and two waiting chairs. The blank inside leaves room for a heartfelt message to someone special.

Product
Page (PDP)

Why you’ll love it

  • A moment of surprise: Open the card to reveal a charming pop-up dining scene that’s sure to bring a smile.
  • Room for your own words: The blank inside gives you space for a short note or a longer letter.
  • Easy to send: The card folds flat and fits in the envelope that comes with it.
  • A thoughtful gift for any occasion: Send it on its own or pair it with a small gift to make someone’s day a little more special.

Packaging

  • Front of the box: Open me. There’s a seat saved for you.
  • Back of the box: Some thank-yous are too big for a text message. This card holds a small table, a warm light, and plenty of space for what you want to say. Take your time.
  • Insert card (inside the box): Thank you for choosing someone special/ Now write the part only you can say.
  • Envelope flap (small line): Made for the people who make room for you.

Social
Captions

  • Instagram (gratitude angle): Some people always save you a seat. Say thank you the way it feels. / Meet Table for Two, the pop-up card that opens to a table for the two of you.
  • Instagram (distance angle): Long-distance doesn’t mean far away. Send a seat at your table.
  • Short post (X or Threads): Not every thank-you needs a speech. Sometimes it just needs a chair.
